Who We Are

Vionet Engineering Pvt Ltd

Vionet Engineering Pvt Ltd delivers innovative, high-performance telecom engineering solutions, helping clients deploy robust, scalable, and future-ready networks across the United States.

Our expertise spans a wide range of services, including FTTx network design, fiber backhaul engineering, Hybrid Fiber-Coaxial (HFC) infrastructure, pole loading analysis, make-ready engineering, and permitting support. We also provide field data collection, field engineering, and the preparation of comprehensive construction-ready drawing packages to ensure smooth project execution.

In the wireless domain, we offer expert services in cell tower design, 5G network construction support, and in-building wireless (IBW/DAS) system design, enabling seamless connectivity and optimized network performance. Backed by deep industry expertise, cutting-edge technology, and a strong understanding of US telecom standards and permitting requirements, Vionet Engineering provides end-to-end solutions covering the complete project lifecycle.

Engineering & Permit Services

At Vionet Engineering, we understand that precision engineering is the foundation of every successful telecom project. Our Engineering & Permit Services cover the entire lifecycle — from early-stage planning to final permit approvals. We take ownership of the process, ensuring that all designs, drafting documents, and traffic plans meet the highest industry standards. With us, your project moves from concept to construction with confidence and efficiency.

Planning

We provide comprehensive project planning services, balancing technical precision with real-world field considerations. Our planners collaborate with engineering and construction teams to deliver optimized, scalable network plans that reduce future costs and deployment risks.

Design

Our experienced designers craft detailed network designs ready for deployment. We utilize the latest tools and adhere to strict design standards, ensuring seamless integration with your existing infrastructure and delivering superior performance.

Drafting

We create high-quality, construction-ready drafting packages that ensure field teams can execute with clarity and accuracy. Our drafts cover route plans, splice diagrams, and construction notes — reducing rework and driving efficiency.

Permitting

We manage complex permitting processes proactively with municipalities and regulatory bodies. Our team delivers complete, accurate permit packages and ensures all required approvals are obtained on time — keeping your project on schedule.

Traffic Control Plan (TCP)

Our traffic control experts design safe, compliant Traffic Control Plans that protect public safety and construction crews. Every TCP developed aligns with local MUTCD guidelines and challenges of each project site.

Turn-Key Network Services

End-to-end Turn-Key Network Services allow clients to focus on core business while we handle technical and logistical complexity. From engineering and permitting to utility relocations, we deliver complete, ready-to-deploy solutions.

Digitization & Base Mapping

We digitize legacy records and create base maps that serve as the foundation for modern network designs. Our precision mapping ensures every line, asset, and easement is captured accurately, supporting engineering and permitting.

Town Planning

Full Plant As-built Walkout

Site Surveys (ISP/OSP)
